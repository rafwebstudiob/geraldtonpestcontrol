Pest control in Cape Burney

Cape Burney is south of Geraldton near the mouth of the Greenough River, with newer homes on larger blocks. Landscaping, garden beds and reticulation around new homes are worth keeping away from the slab edge, so the edge stays dry and visible for inspection.

Termites

Geraldton's dry, winter-rain climate doesn't rule termites out. Moisture from leaks, sprinklers and garden beds against walls is what often draws them to a house. Have the home inspected regularly and check for mud tubes, hollow timber and damp areas. Read the Geraldton termite guide and what to expect from an inspection.

Licensing

In Western Australia, pest management businesses must be registered with the Department of Health, and treatments must be carried out by a licensed pest management technician under the Health (Pesticides) Regulations 2011. The technician's licence card lists endorsements, such as Commercial Domestic Pests or Termites and Timber pests, that show what they can treat. See how to check a licence.
